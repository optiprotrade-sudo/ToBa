# First 7 days: launch both freelancing paths

## Day 1: Get visible
- Choose the name you want clients to see and a professional email address.
- Open `index.html` and personalize your identity and contact link.
- Confirm exactly what work you can demonstrate or deliver yourself.

## Day 2: Portfolio proof
- Add at least two real screenshots from your own projects.
- Write brief notes about the exact features you worked on, tools used and current status.
- Do a full mobile + desktop check.

## Day 3: Publish
- Deploy the static folder to Netlify (or GitHub Pages).
- Test every navigation and email link using the public site URL.
- Check how it looks on your phone.

## Day 4: Upwork
- Create/complete a freelancer account and verify identity if prompted.
- Use the profile draft from this pack and link your portfolio.
- Review job leads, selecting only jobs within your ability and checking Connects costs.

## Day 5: Fiverr
- On a desktop browser, activate seller mode and complete onboarding.
- Create one focused landing-page gig and a gallery image based on your actual work.
- Set prices based on scope, not just the cheapest competitors.

## Day 6: Outreach
- Send 3 personalized proposals to well-matched jobs that are still open.
- Send 3 respectful, personalized introductions to businesses whose websites could genuinely benefit from your services.
- Record responses in your own notes.

## Day 7: Improve
- Review what opportunities attracted interest.
- Improve the portfolio presentation, samples and proposals.
- Plan small, clearly defined tasks for the following week.

## Target
Get your portfolio online, your profiles published and your first legitimate client conversation. Earnings are possible, not guaranteed.
