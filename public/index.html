05 / GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

A few quick answers before you tell us what you're building.

Can you work with clients outside Nigeria?

Yes. ToBa accepts project enquiries from around the world. We can coordinate remotely and confirm the working hours and payment arrangement before a project begins.

Do I need to have a design or technical plan already?

No. Share the idea and any examples you like. We can clarify the scope and agree on the deliverables before development begins.

Do the starting prices include hosting and a domain?

Not by default. Any hosting, domain or paid third-party service costs will be discussed separately before you approve the work.

Can you fix an existing website rather than create a new one?

Yes. Describe the issue, share a public link if available and explain what you expect to happen. ToBa can review whether the requested fix is within scope.
