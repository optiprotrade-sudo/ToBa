## Before you click Apply

- Check if the job is still accepting proposals.
- Don't bid for React/WordPress/Shopify work unless you can demonstrably deliver it.
- Check client payment verification and job history where shown.
- Don't say you reviewed a reference you haven't actually visited.
- Don't start work on Upwork before a contract and appropriately funded milestone are in place.
- Edit every proposal to match the client's actual request; avoid identical mass submissions.
